Turn long videos into short, ready-to-post clips

ClipFlow is a Windows desktop app that transcribes your video, finds the moments worth clipping, applies a crop/watermark/caption template, and publishes the result straight to TikTok — without your source footage ever leaving your machine.

What ClipFlow does

Everything happens on your own computer, from importing the source video to queuing the finished clip for TikTok.

▶

Import & transcribe

Drop in a long-form video or pull one in directly from YouTube. ClipFlow transcribes the audio so it knows what's being said, when.

✨

AI highlight detection

Transcript excerpts are sent to an AI service to surface the strongest moments — the app suggests where a clip should start and end.

✂

Trim & preview

Fine-tune in/out points with an FFmpeg-backed trimmer and play the exact clip back before you commit to a render.

▣

Template editor

Design a reusable crop, watermark, and caption-style template on a visual canvas, then apply it consistently across clips.

↑

Queue & auto-publish

Connect a TikTok account and queue a rendered clip for direct posting — ClipFlow tracks upload progress and status to completion.

🔒

Local-first & private

Video files, transcripts, and captions are stored locally in a SQLite database. Only text excerpts leave the device, for captioning.

How it works

From a long video to a published clip, in four steps.

Import a video

Load a local file or import from YouTube.

Pick the moment

Review AI-suggested highlights or trim manually.

Apply a template

Crop, watermark, and caption in one pass.

Publish

Queue it to your connected TikTok account.

🔒

Your footage stays on your device

ClipFlow doesn't upload your video files anywhere. See exactly what's processed locally and what (only transcript text, for AI captioning) ever leaves your machine in our Privacy Policy.
