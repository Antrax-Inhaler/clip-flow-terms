What ClipFlow does

Everything happens on your own computer, from importing the source video to queuing the finished clip for TikTok.

▶

Import & transcribe

Drop in a long-form video or pull one in directly from YouTube. ClipFlow transcribes the audio so it knows what's being said, when.

✨

AI highlight detection

Transcript excerpts are sent to an AI service to surface the strongest moments — the app suggests where a clip should start and end.

✂

Trim & preview

Fine-tune in/out points with an FFmpeg-backed trimmer and play the exact clip back before you commit to a render.

▣

Template editor

Design a reusable crop, watermark, and caption-style template on a visual canvas, then apply it consistently across clips.

↑

Queue & auto-publish

Connect a TikTok account and queue a rendered clip for direct posting — ClipFlow tracks upload progress and status to completion.

🔒

Local-first & private

Video files, transcripts, and captions are stored locally in a SQLite database. Only text excerpts leave the device, for captioning.

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions about how ClipFlow handles your video and accounts.

Does ClipFlow upload my video files anywhere?

No. Source videos, transcripts, and rendered clips are processed and stored locally in a SQLite database on your machine. Only short transcript text excerpts are sent out, and only to generate captions and highlight suggestions.

What formats can I import?

Standard long-form video files, plus direct import from a YouTube URL. Clips are rendered with FFmpeg, so common web-friendly codecs and containers are supported.

How does publishing to TikTok work?

You connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own Login Kit consent screen. ClipFlow never sees your TikTok password — it only receives an access token, used to queue and publish the clip you choose via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Can I disconnect a connected account?

Yes. Connected accounts and their stored tokens live locally and can be removed from ClipFlow's Accounts page at any time, which also revokes ClipFlow's ability to publish on your behalf going forward.

Is ClipFlow free?

ClipFlow is currently in private beta. Pricing and availability details will be announced as the app moves toward a public release.
